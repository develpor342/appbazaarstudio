Deen360 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · App Bazaar Studio

This Privacy Policy describes how Deen360 (“the App”), published by App Bazaar Studio, handles information. By using the App, you agree to this policy. For website practices, see our Website Privacy Policy.

Prayer Times & Qibla Location

Deen360 provides prayer times and Qibla direction. To calculate accurate timings and Qibla bearing, the App may use location-related data when you grant permission.

  • Location may be obtained from device GPS, network-based location, or a city/manual location you select.
  • Location is used to compute prayer schedules and Qibla direction — not to build marketing profiles for App Bazaar Studio.
  • You can deny or revoke location permission in system settings; some features may then rely on manual city selection or become limited.

App Features & Local Content

Content such as Quran reading progress, favorites, or preferences may be stored locally on your device. We do not require you to share personal worship data with us to use core offline-capable features.

Advertising (AdMob)

The App may display advertisements through Google AdMob or similar Google advertising services. These services may collect device and usage information to show and measure ads, subject to Google’s policies.

  • Google Advertising Privacy
  • Google Privacy & Terms

Children’s Privacy

Parents and guardians are welcome to use Deen360 with their families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 for our own databases. If you believe such information was collected, contact us so we can delete it.
